About me

Hi! I’m Xiaoyue Xiong, pronounced like Shiaoyweh Shyong — every x in Chinese sounds like sh. I’m a Ph.D. candidate at The Pennsylvania State University.

My work brings individual development happening at different scales into one framework, such as estimating long-term trends and short-term fluctuation jointly, with heterogeneity between people built in rather than averaged out. Broadly, I’m interested in how variables influence one another within a person, how people influence one another, and how any quantitative model can inform the work around it: designing studies and planning power beforehand, and understanding, classifying, predicting and intervening afterwards.

I’m always happy to talk and collaborate, whether you’re into quantitative methodology or applied research. Feel free to reach out to me.
