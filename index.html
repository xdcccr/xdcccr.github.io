I develop longitudinal models for long-term change (trends) and short-term fluctuation (dynamics) that account properly for heterogeneity between people.

I also work on other longitudinal models, reliability analysis, and machine learning methods and their applications to individual development, prevention and intervention, bullying among children and adolescents, and general human development and family study areas.

Software & Codes

BurstiVAR

R · JAGS · MIT licence

Multilevel VAR for measurement burst designs, together with GoBurstiVAR, a nested parametric variant that replaces the free burst intercepts with a Gompertz growth function. The repository holds the full parameter-recovery and efficiency simulations, the empirical analysis, the convergence diagnostics behind the appendix tables, the figure scripts, and two runnable tutorials — the BurstiVAR one finishes in about a minute.

Repository

mlGAR

R · JAGS

Multilevel growth with autoregressive residuals: a Gompertz trend and a multilevel AR(1) process estimated together in one Bayesian model, rather than detrending first and modelling the residuals afterwards. It recovers parameters with as few as five time points per person, where two-stage approaches break down. Includes a tutorial vignette covering data generation, model specification, sampling and diagnostics.

Repository The paper

gradual-abrupt-changes-bayesian

Co-authored · maintained by Yanling Li

Simulation code, shared utilities and vignettes behind the Bayesian regime-switching framework and the entropy measure used to represent and test gradual and abrupt change together. I am a co-author on the paper; the repository is Yanling Li's.

Repository The paper Talk (video)

dynr

R · C · on CRAN · maintained by Michael D. Hunter

Dynamic models with regime switching. I built and presented a stochastic approximation EM (SAEM) routine for fitting differential equation models with random effects in dynr (ISDSA, 2022). The package itself is the work of Michael D. Hunter, Sy-Miin Chow and the dynr team.
